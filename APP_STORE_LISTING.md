## Description (4000 characters max)

Memorezar is a real-time memorization assistant that helps you memorize quotes, speeches, poetry, scripture, and any text you want to commit to memory.

HOW IT WORKS

Open the app, add the text you want to memorize, and start reciting out loud. Memorezar listens to every word and alerts you the instant you make a mistake — not 4 or 5 words later, but right away. This immediate feedback helps you learn faster by correcting errors before they become habits.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY

• Voice Mode — Recite out loud and get real-time mistake detection
• First Letter Mode — See just the first letter of each word as a hint
• Multiple Choice Mode — Pick the correct next word from options

INSTANT FEEDBACK

When you say the wrong word, Memorezar alerts you immediately with:
• Audio alerts — Choose from 10+ sounds (buzzer, chime, drum hit, and more)
• Visual flash — A screen flash highlights the mistake
• Haptic vibration — Feel the correction on supported devices

Mix and match alert types to suit your preference.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS

• See your practice streak, accuracy, and mastered quotes at a glance
• Each quote tracks individual accuracy and session count
• Watch yourself improve from Beginner to Mastered

ORGANIZE YOUR LIBRARY

• Add your own quotes, poems, prayers, or speeches
• Browse curated suggestion packs to discover new material
• Create custom categories with cover photos
• Split long texts into manageable chunks or merge short ones

WORKS OFFLINE

All speech recognition and practice happens on your device. No internet connection required. Your data never leaves your phone.

CUSTOMIZABLE

• Adjustable font sizes for comfortable reading
• Light, Dark, or System theme
• Control word visibility — show all, hide until spoken, or partial reveal
• Choose your preferred mistake and completion sounds

Whether you're a student memorizing material for school, a performer learning lines, or someone who wants to memorize meaningful quotes and scripture — Memorezar is your personal practice partner.

## App Store Review Notes

This app requires microphone and speech recognition permissions to function. During review, please:

1. Add any text (e.g., "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog")
2. Tap the quote to enter recitation mode
3. Tap the microphone button and begin reciting
4. Intentionally say a wrong word to hear/see the mistake alert

The app works entirely offline. No login or account is required.
