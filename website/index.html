Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Memorezar respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Memorezar website and mobile application, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Memorezar may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, login credentials, or profile details.
  • Practice content you choose to upload, enter, or save inside the app, such as passages, prayers, quotations, study material, or notes.
  • Voice-related interaction data necessary to provide memorization and listening features.
  • Usage information, such as session activity, device type, operating system, app version, crash data, and general analytics.
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests and feedback.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Memorezar app and website.
  • Enable memorization, listening, review, correction, and progress-related features.
  • Personalize your experience and save your preferences.
  • Respond to support inquiries and communicate important updates.
  • Monitor performance, troubleshoot bugs, and protect the security of the service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Voice data and spoken practice

Because Memorezar is built around spoken memorization, certain voice-related data may be processed to deliver listening, alignment, and correction features. Where applicable, voice input may be processed by Memorezar and/or trusted service providers strictly for app functionality, improvement, safety, and support. You should adjust this section to match your actual technical implementation.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in limited cases, such as:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, storage, customer support, or speech-processing providers.
  • When required by law, legal process, or governmental request.
  • To protect rights, safety, security, users, or the public.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

6. Your choices

  • You may be able to access, update, or delete certain account information from within the app.
  • You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated data — see Section 7 below.
  • You can control certain device permissions, including microphone access, through your device settings.
  • You may opt out of certain non-essential communications.

7. Account deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Memorezar account and all associated data at any time, through either of the following channels:

  • In the app: Open Memorezar → Settings → Account → Delete My Account. You'll be signed out immediately, and your deletion request is queued.
  • By email: If you no longer have access to the app, email support@memorezar.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include the email address associated with your Memorezar account. Where possible, please send from that same email address so we can verify the request.

Once we receive a deletion request through either channel, we will permanently delete your Memorezar account, cloud backups, and any community recordings you've shared within 30 days. Deletion is irreversible. After deletion, signing in with the same email through any provider will create a new, empty account.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. By using the service, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed in such locations, subject to applicable safeguards where required.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may notify users by updating the date above, posting a notice in the app or website, or by other appropriate means.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us at privacy@memorezar.com.
