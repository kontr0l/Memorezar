# Privacy Policy for Memorezar

**Last Updated: March 12, 2026**

## Overview

Memorezar ("the App") is a memorization practice tool developed by Jalil. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

**Memorezar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All data created while using the App — including your quotes, practice statistics, settings, and recordings — is stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

## Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, which are used solely for the described purposes:

- **Microphone**: Used to listen to your voice during recitation practice so the App can detect mistakes in real time. Audio is processed on-device using Apple's Speech framework and is never recorded or transmitted.
- **Speech Recognition**: Used to convert your spoken words into text for comparison against the quote you are memorizing. All recognition is performed on-device.
- **Camera** (optional): Used only if you choose to take a photo as a cover image for a quote category. Photos are stored locally on your device.

## Third-Party Services

- **Unsplash** (optional): If you search for a category cover photo, the App connects to the Unsplash API to fetch images. This is the only network request made by the core App. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to that service: https://unsplash.com/privacy
- **Apple Speech Framework**: Speech recognition is performed on-device. No audio data is sent to Apple for this purpose.

## Data Storage

All App data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. You can delete all App data at any time from the Settings screen within the App, or by deleting the App from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Memorezar does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The App does not require account creation or collect any identifying information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
